# Film Room

A film-study tool for ultimate frisbee teams. Break down YouTube footage together: tag clips, draw on frames, build highlight reels and scouting reports.

**Open it:** https://richard-schnitter.github.io/film-room/

## What it does

- **Three sections:** Opponents (scouting), Our team (self-scout: clips marked *to fix* or *good example*, per-player feedback reels) and Tactics (any other footage worth learning from).
- **Tagging:** `I` / `O` mark a clip (**Cancel clip** or `Esc` throws it away), then tag it (their O or D, stack, force, zone, huck, line ratio…), add players, a priority and a note.
- **Drawing:** `D` pauses on the frame. Draw cuts, throws, zones and O/D markers. Playback stops on drawings so everyone sees them.
- **Team notes:** anyone can add general notes to a team (how they play in wind, who their captains are, what the coaches said). Each note shows who wrote it and when, and they appear in the copied report. Long notes show the first few lines; tap one to expand it, and tap **Notes** to fold the whole list away. **Voice notes:** in *Add note*, upload a voice note a friend sent (WhatsApp .opus, .m4a, .mp3 and so on) and it is turned into text right on your phone or laptop, free. Afrikaans voice notes become an English note, with the Afrikaans kept underneath; pick **Best** on a laptop for Afrikaans, since the phone-sized models are weak at it. The audio is saved with the note (up to 4 MB) and anyone on the team can press **Listen**.
- **Watching:** ±1, ±5 and +20 second jumps, the arrow keys jump 10 seconds, and **CC** turns on subtitles (YouTube's auto-generated ones when the video has no others). YouTube does not let other sites set the picture quality, so **Quality** (the gear on phones) switches to YouTube's own controls: pick the quality from its gear menu. Keep it on while watching, since turning it off resets the quality.
- **Team profiles:** tap a team's name (or **Profile**) to see everything on them in one place: the game plan, every note in full (with any Afrikaans original and the voice note to listen to), what they get tagged for (tap a tag to watch just those clips), their players and their games. **Full screen** spreads it across the whole screen in columns; `Esc` or **Exit full screen** goes back.
- **Player profiles:** everything on one player in one place: tendencies, strengths, weaknesses, how to stop them, what they get tagged for most, and every clip across all games. *Follow while watching* tags every new clip with that player during a deep dive.
- **Game stats:** press **Record stats** next to *Edit game* and the notes panel becomes a stat sheet for the game you are watching, so the video never leaves the screen. Say whether you received or pulled, pick the line (your saved lines and the last O and D line are one tap), start the point, then tap players as the disc moves: a tap is a pick-up, a catch or a block, and **Goal**, **Drop**, **Throwaway**, **Stall**, **They turned it over** and **They scored** cover the rest. **Undo** takes back the last tap. Every tap is stamped with the video time. **Box score** adds it up for the game or for all your games (holds, breaks, and each player's points, plus/minus, goals, assists, throws, completion, turnovers and blocks); tap any figure to list the plays behind it and tap a play to watch it. Stats are shared with the team like everything else and need no change to the database rules.
- **Stats page:** the **Stats** tab shows everything recorded so far, for one game or all games: a plain-language summary, hold, dirty-hold and break rates, leaders, a sortable player table, turnovers by type and player, who throws to whom, how long possessions run, hockey assists, lines and pairs with their record, and how the other team's possessions end. Any player's numbers open the plays behind them on video.
- **Pulls, defence and the other team:** while recording a point you can also log who pulled, the hang time (typed, or timed with a button), where the pull landed, person or zone defence after the pull and after each turnover, who stood where in the zone, each pass of theirs, how they lost the disc and which match-up lost it, a throw of ours that was blocked, and where on the field a turnover happened. The Stats page turns these into a *Pulls and defence* tab (hang time, bricks, landing spot and defence against the break rate, zone positions, a table per puller), a fuller *Opposition* tab (their passes per possession, scoring by possession length, how and who loses it), turnovers by field position, chains of three passes and each player share of the points played.
- **Import from Ultimate Tracker:** the **Import** button also reads an Ultimate Tracker game file. Players are matched to your roster by name (or added), each game goes onto a Film Room game you pick or onto a new game without video, and the points appear on the Stats page. A game without video shows its stats and can be given a YouTube link later under *Edit game*.
- **Stats tab and one-player tracking:** stats have their own **Stats** tab beside Scouting report. *One player* follows a single player on any team with no roster or line: add just that player, then log each throw (type, distance, open or break side, who it went to, how it ended) plus goals caught, blocks, drops and stalls, each stamped with the video time. A summary shows completion by throw type, distance and side and their favourite targets, for one game or all games. *Games* lists every game that has stats and can list a game under your own team. A goal the player catches can record where the cut started. On a computer in full screen, **Stats** (or the S key) opens the stat sheet as a movable window over the video, and everything recorded is marked on an S lane of the timeline.
- **Highlight reels:** filter by team, player, tag or priority and play only those clips back to back. Each clip's note and who wrote it show across the top of the video and in the *Playing* box.
- **Reports:** per-team summaries. Tap any timestamp or note to play that moment in the video. The copied report has links that open YouTube at the right second.
- **Playbook:** draw your own plays on a field (attackers, defenders, the disc, runs, throws, zones and labels) in up to 8 steps, then press play to watch them move. Tag a clip with a play and the film shows up next to the drawing; reels can filter by play, and a team's profile and report list the plays that matter against them.
- **Coaching hub:** 21 pages on ultimate strategy (offence, defence, game day), a library of 40 drills and 8 ready-made practice plans, 21 of them with moving diagrams you can copy into the playbook. One search box covers everything; drills can be filtered by skill and by how many players you have, and every strategy page links to the drills that train it. Each page has an *Our team's take* box shared with the team, and shows your own clips that carry the matching tags.
- **Home:** the first page everyone sees: what they have been asked to watch, the clips teammates tagged them in, replies to their comments and what is new.
- **Game plans:** one printable page per opponent with the keys to the game, match-ups against their key players, must-watch clips, the plays to run and your lines.
- **AI brief:** on an opponent's game plan, *Write the brief with AI* sends everything recorded on that team (notes, clips and comments, player profiles, stats, your roster and playbook) to Claude and gets back a brief the team can study: how they play offence and defence with the clips that show it, key players and how to stop them, keys to the game, suggested match-ups and plays, the clips to watch first, what is still unknown, and a message for the team chat with video links. It uses an Anthropic API key that one person pastes under *AI set-up*; the key is kept in that browser only (never in this repository or the team room) and the finished brief is shared with everyone.
- **Team:** *Assignments* (ask people to watch a game or its clips by a date; progress fills in from what they watch), *Roster and lines* (your own players, who is available, the lines you call and their W/M ratio; **Import CSV** adds the whole club at once from a file or pasted rows of name, shirt number and gender, and updates anyone already on the roster instead of adding them twice), *Practices* (your own sessions built from the drill library, with attendance) and *Activity*.
- **Comments on clips:** any clip can carry a thread, and replies show up under the bell and on Home.
- **Team activity:** one page that shows what each teammate has added (clips, notes, plays) and what they have watched: film time, how much of each game, which clips and which Coaching hub pages. *By game* shows who has watched a game and who has not. Watch history is shared through the team room, everyone on the team can see it, and it only counts from the day the page was added.
- **Team sync:** with a Firebase project configured, everything anyone tags appears for the whole team live. Everyone tags the same games, and *Whose clips* shows one person's markers or everyone's. Without Firebase, use Export / Import to swap notes.
- **Phones:** tabs at the bottom, the video stays pinned while you scroll the notes, and big buttons throughout. **Full screen** works on iPhone and Android: turn the phone sideways, tap to show or hide the controls, double-tap the sides to jump 5 seconds, drag the timeline to scrub, and mark clips or draw without leaving full screen. It can also be added to the home screen as an app.

## Who can see the notes

Everyone signs in with Google; there is no other way in. The link is the same for the whole team (https://richard-schnitter.github.io/film-room/) and opens a sign-in page. The organiser (whoever started the room) presses **Team** and adds each teammate's Google email. After that, the teammate signs in and lands straight in the team's film. Someone whose email is not on the list sees "no team has added you yet" and can copy their email to send to the organiser. Removing someone from the list locks them out. Nothing the team tags is ever deleted from the database; removed clips are marked as removed.

## Setting up live team sync

1. Create a Firebase project with a Firestore database.
2. In Authentication, turn on the **Google** sign-in provider and add the site's domain under **Authorized domains**.
3. Publish the rules in [`firestore.rules`](firestore.rules) as the Firestore rules. Publish them again whenever that file changes.
4. Paste the project's web config into `FIREBASE_CONFIG` in `index.html`.
